About

I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Bhubaneswar, India. I joined in January 2023. Before that I was a Research Associate at Universität Hamburg, Germany (November 2019 to December 2022), working with Prof. Dr. Chris Biemann.

I completed my Ph.D. at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Kharagpur, under Prof. Dr. Pawan Goyal. My M.Tech is also from IIT Kharagpur, where my thesis was supervised by Prof. Dr. Anupam Basu. My B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is from the Institute of Engineering and Management, Kolkata.
